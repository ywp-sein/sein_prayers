# 73 Painting colors

*21.10.2026*

"...What if, instead, the moments that seem most impossible,<br>
we dared to lift our hopes higher still—<br>
not on the frail wings of human wishing,<br>
but on the unshakable promises of His Word?<br>
What if the boundless goodness we know of Him<br>
became the canvas for the brightest vision our hearts can hold?... "<br>
-- Yuan-Wei Pi, Surpassing Expectation

This is the 73rd weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

I thank You for Your kindness and for Your willingness to give us calmness, peace, and stillness. Father, I thank You for inviting us into a deeper peace, helping us let go of the things we hold onto so tightly, and removing the things that continue to trouble us.

Lord, please give us the wisdom to articulate our experiences with You, and to share Your goodness through our testimonies. We are living testimonies. As long as we walk with You, there will always be more and more testimonies to share.

Give us the wisdom to write them down, to share them, and even to live out Your grace and mercy in our lives. Transform our hearts into what You want them to be, so that we can perceive more of Your goodness and recognize what You are trying to teach us.

Jesus, You are the way, the truth, and the life. Prompt us to continue drinking the living water and to keep planting seeds, even when we cannot see the outcome with our own eyes. Increase our faith. Help us trust that You know all things, that You are sovereign, and that You are good.

And Holy Spirit, please help us paint the abundant colors of Your goodness throughout our lives—the color of hope, the color of grace, the color of love, and the color of peace. May these colors spread throughout the city in unexpected and creative ways.

Help us to become vessels through which Your beautiful colors are revealed. May the way we live, love, create, speak, and serve bring Your colors into the places around us, so that people may encounter Your goodness and Your love in ways they did not expect.

In Your holy name, I pray.

Amen.
