# 54 Cast the Burdens on the Lord

*10.06.2026*

A verse is shared with me after the Bible study today:

Cast your burden on the LORD , And He shall sustain you; He shall never permit the righteous to be moved.
Psalms 55:22

"The promise is not that He will take away the pressure, but that He will hold us up under it; and, similarly, the last clause declares that the righteous will not be allowed to stumble. Faith is mentioned before righteousness. The two must go together; for trust which is not accompanied and manifested by righteousness is no true trust, and righteousness which is not grounded in trust is no stable or real righteousness."
--Alexander Maclaren on Psalm 55:22 

This is the 54th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father, 
Thank You for encouraging me through Your Word through a sister. I love the verse, and I trust Your promises. Please strengthen my faith in You, strengthen Your children in this city, and constantly remind us that You are strong and kind, as we sang together tonight. Help us to stay in Your presence, for You are good and faithful.

Thank You for Your willingness to keep us day and night. Help us to believe that no one else can be our shield and strength. Teach us how to follow You and let You alone be our direction. Do not allow us to solve problems by ourselves. Forge our characters to submit to Your will and believe that You use everything perfectly for the one who truly loves You. And let Your perfect love drive out fear, so that we won't be shaken. 

In Jesus’ precious and mighty name, I pray and receive.
Amen.
