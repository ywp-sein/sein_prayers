The is the 74th weekly prayer, 

Dear Heavenly Father,

I thank You for the good news that our broken past can be redeemed, that the lost can be redeemed, and that even the time we have lost can be redeemed because of You. Thank You for being our Redeemer and our Savior.

Help us to believe that You will make all things new, even in the wilderness; that a fountain of life will flow; and that countless fruits will be borne.

Lord, please speak to Your children in this city and ask them:

“Do you believe that a great harvest is coming, even in a season of famine?”

“Do you believe that the hope of sunrise is coming, even in the darkness of the night?”

“Do you believe that there is a brighter future and a glorious eternity ahead, even though we cannot yet see them with our own eyes?”

“Do you believe that a great revival and revelation are coming to this city, even though it may seem that everyone is still going their own way?”

“Do you believe in a mighty God who can change everything?”

“Do you believe that God is the One who can do the impossible—things beyond our imagination, our expectations, and even our human desires?”

“Do you believe that there is a great and glorious home in Heaven, where we can be with Him forever and ever?”

“Do you believe that your name is written in the Book of Life, and that in eternity you will experience no more sorrow, pain, or suffering?”

“Do you believe that all these things are true?”

“Do you believe that Jesus Christ is your Lord and Savior, and do you acknowledge Him not only with your words, but also through your deeds?”

May we believe. May we trust. May we hold on to the hope You have given us, and may we live our lives faithfully as Your followers.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

Amen.
