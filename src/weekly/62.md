# 62 Wonderful Counselor

*05.08.2026*

"View our lives through the vast macro
Of His eternal purpose sublime.
Trusting Him fully in every micro
Along the faithful path He has prepared.

Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God,
Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace—
Holy Majesty beyond all names and all praise,
Yet stooping low to call me beloved."
Grace for the Beloved, Yuan-Wei Pi 

This is the 62nd weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Thank You for being our Wonderful Counselor.
Thank You for being our Mighty God. 
Thank You for being our Everlasting Father. 
Thank You for being our Prince of Peace. 

May Your peace dwell in Your children's hearts, who are willing to sit and stay in Your presence, waiting to see more of Your wonderful plans, mighty works, everlasting love, and the Gospel of peace transforming people in this city.

Please show us more of Your humor, and teach us how to enjoy Your humor, and even be part of some oopsy-checkmate moments to the devil. You are so funny, and I love You very much.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 
Amen.
