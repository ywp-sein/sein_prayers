# 72 Confidence

*14.10.2026*

Yesterday, we had a Bible study. During the sharing and prayer, a sister felt led to share Hebrews 10:35–36 to encourage me:
“Therefore do not throw away your confidence, which has a great reward. For you have need of endurance, so that when you have done the will of God you may receive what is promised.”

She then continued with Hebrews 11:1: “Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see.”

Before bed, I saw that today’s verse on YouVersion is: “But blessed is the one who trusts in the Lord, whose confidence is in him.” (Jeremiah 17:7)

Okay, Father, thank You for Your encouragement.

This is the 72nd weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Thank You for Your encouragement, and thank You for Your words and faithfulness.

Help us to pray for impossible things. Help us to believe that soon You will deliver our enemies into our hands. May all sorts of evil tactics, false ideologies, and false doctrines be removed from this city. And soon, people will know that there is a mighty God among us in this city, who does great things and impossible things.

Lord, I know the harvest is ready, so I ask for more laborers who are willing to do Your will with Your strength alone. Help us to share our testimonies in creative ways that bring light to people.

Please remove our fears, give us more confidence, and strengthen our faith by helping us firmly know who You are, not by what we see. Help us to live by faith and close every door to turning back. I believe we don't need to turn back. Please simply help us to surrender and eliminate even the concept of turning back in us.

May You be our only confidence.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 
Amen.
