# 52 Pure 

*28.05.2026*

"You can no more have the Holy Ghost and have self-love than you can have purity and impurity at the same moment in the same place."
 — How to Be Filled with the Holy Spirit, A.W. Tozer

"Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God."
Matthew 5:8

The is the 52nd weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father, 

Please purify our hearts, keep us in Your love, and help us not to compromise or be conformed to this world. Help us to see even more clearly the line between holiness and immorality.

Please give us hearts to serve and to believe that Your grace is sufficient for us to live sacrificial lives. Please help us to be like Paul, making ourselves servants to the people around us, that we might win more of them.

Please purify Your churches in this city. Help us to be united through Your Spirit, based on Your Word. I ask for reformation and revival in this city: a return to the teaching of Scripture and lives brought into proper relationship with the Holy Spirit.

Thank You, Lord, that You prayed and continue to pray for our unity. Please continue to bless Your Bride, the Church.

May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Ghost, be with the Church, forever and ever.

Amen.
