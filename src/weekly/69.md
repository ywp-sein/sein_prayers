# 69 Awaken the City Through Your Beauty 

*23.09.2026*

I went to a birthday party a few days ago. Besides hearing beautiful testimonies from different countries, I witnessed a beautiful view when we went up to the rooftop. There was even a shooting star as I climbed up to the roof. I think this is one of the most beautiful pictures I’ve taken in Berlin.

This is the 69th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Thank You for what You have done in the city. You have planted so many seeds, and You have poured down Your grace to help them grow. Father, I ask for more of Your grace and mercy to continue watering the people in the city who turn to You.

Please help people see what You are doing in the city, so that they may see and taste Your grace and know that You are good. Please remove the veils from people's eyes, especially from those who could not see You before but could only see the world through their own reflections. Lord, I ask You to give them new insight and a new perspective, and to bring the light of hope into their brokenness.

Father, please pour down warmth and compassion within people's hearts, so that they will be willing to help and share, not for their own benefit, but simply out of a genuine desire to help others. I also ask the same for those who receive help, that they may also cultivate a heart to help others.

Please cultivate and forge this character throughout the city, so that people may naturally help one another without calculating their own benefits. For those who constantly calculate what they can gain, Lord, please reveal the truth to them and help them recognize their selfishness. And if they are unwilling to help others in the future, Lord, I ask You to lead them away from the city.

Father, I also pray that Your goodness and greatness will be manifested throughout the city, and that more and more people will come to know how much You love this city.

Jesus, please awaken the city through Your beauty.

In Your holy name I pray,

Amen.
