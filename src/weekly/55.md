# 55 Do it Again and More

*17.06.2026*

“Self must not only be dead but buried out of sight, for the stench of the unburied self-life will frighten souls away from Jesus.”
--John Nelson Hyde

This is the 55th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Please highlight the ignorance in our lives,<br>
and give us the courage to face it directly through Your grace.


Please bless Your true churches in this city,<br>
that true disciples may be multiplied,<br>
for they stand firm in the Truth amidst Babylon.


Please tear down the communities in this city that use Your name in vain,<br>
and show them whom they are actually worshipping.


And Father, I want to echo the prayer that was uttered thousands of years ago:<br>
"Lord, I have heard of Your fame;<br>
I stand in awe of Your deeds, Lord.<br>
Repeat them in our day,<br>
in our time make them known;<br>
in wrath remember mercy."

We have heard the stories of reformation and revival; please do it again in Berlin. 


And please, let it not be a fleeting heartbeat or a passing flame in the night, but a lasting work of Your hand— a living river that does not run dry, and a miracle that endures from generation to generation.

May Your children in this city be filled with the Holy Spirit to speak the Word of God with boldness, until Your name alone is praised.

Hallowed be Thy name.

In Your holy name I pray and receive, 
Amen.
