# 61 Stay in Awe of God  

*29.07.2026*

Looking back at the business proposal I wrote 1.5 years ago, I can only say that the Lord has been incredibly gracious to me. Moreover, at the beginning of this year (#32), I prayed for being more articulate. Through prayer and negotiation, God also provided me with this drone without me spending a single penny.

I don't need any more evidence that He is a faithful God, yet once again, He has reminded me of His faithfulness. What matters even more is growing in gratitude toward the One who has always been faithful to me and gaining a deeper understanding of how God answers prayers.

As long as I remember what I prayed for, I can always see the connections between those prayers. Those cross-references are one of the ways God speaks to me and communicates His faithfulness.

This is the 61st weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Thank You for always being in control. Together with the solid truth that You are my Father, You have replaced the anxiety in me with Your peace. And You have not only replaced it but have also enhanced, and are still enhancing the peace in me.

Thank You for teaching me how to pray, as I always love to ask You to teach me how to pray according to Your will, so that I may be used for Your will. And thank You for showing me that whatever I ask in Your name, with more and more understanding of Your name and as I hallow Your name, I can simply receive through faith.

Help me to stay in awe of You, so that I can continue to hallow Your name and say, "May Your will be done," in my heart. I also ask You the same for Your children in this city. Please continue to teach them how to pray according to Your will, not according to their desires, so that they can experience more of Your greatness and faithfulness and glorify You more with their pure hearts.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 
Amen.
