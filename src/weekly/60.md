# 60 Timely 

*22.07.2026*

A miracle just happened today. A dear friend encouraged me to pray and negotiate the compensation for what has happened to me, and it is truly that I had to wait until this moment and have the courage and wisdom to negotiate. Once again, I witnessed God's perfect timing. It seems that we can enjoy the outcome together before the end of summer. I will pray for good weather. 

This is the 60th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

I thank You for Your patience in enduring my impatience and pulling me back from impulsive decisions. And I thank You that I can come boldly to You and find grace to help me in time of need. Help me to stay in awe of You, for You said that those who fear You lack nothing, and therefore I shall not want. 

Thank You that I can testify to Your love and the faithfulness of Your Word through my own life. I pray that more people in the city can taste and see that You are good and know that they can take refuge in You. 

Help us to understand Your timing and endure patiently only through Your grace, while still believing that we can ask for Your compassion to satisfy us early with Your mercy, because You are our Father in heaven, and we are Your children. 

Help us to stand firm in our identity in You and only in You, and wait for the wonders with which You want to spoil Your children in Your joy. Thank You for loving us so much. 

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 

Amen.
