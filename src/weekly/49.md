# 49 God Has a Plan

*06.05.2026*

Recently I got a concordance to the Bible just by initiating a question, and God brought more fruits from that. Today, while I was queuing for food, the sister next to me shared that some words carry deeper meanings. For example, “life” in certain contexts does not necessarily refer to length of days, but to abundance.


This is the 49th weekly prayer:


"What we need as a nation more than anything else is more gratitude, not more grievance."
Former Republican senator, Ben Sasse 

"It's not a suprise to God. God has a plan, absolutely."
Stage 4 pancreatic cancer fighter, Ben Sasse 

Dear Heavenly Father,

I just want to thank You for everything that has happened in my life, and with the confidence You have given me, I thank You in advance for everything that will happen in my life.

I pray for more gratitude and less grievance in the city. I know You have a great plan for this city. 

May Your name be known to all nations.

To the only wise God be glory forevermore through Jesus Christ.

Amen.
