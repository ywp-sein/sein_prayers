# 63 The Discernment of the Heart

*12.08.2026*

"Let him then, who would be indeed a Christian, watch over his ways and over his heart with unceasing circumspection."<br>
― William Wilberforce, Real Christianity

"Bringing every thought into captivity to the obedience of Christ"<br>
― 2 Corinthians 10:5b

This is the 63rd weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Have mercy on Your beloved, search our hearts and test our minds, for You understand us more deeply than we understand ourselves. Humble us, teach us, and help us to faithfully guard our hearts.

Examine us and prove us; test our minds and our hearts. Let Your lovingkindness remain before our eyes, and help us walk faithfully in Your truth.

Give grace to those who turn to You. Help us cast away every thought that does not please You, and teach us to meditate on whatever is true, noble, just, pure, lovely, commendable, virtuous, and praiseworthy. Help us to recognize that every good and beautiful thing comes from You, and make us continually thankful for Your goodness.

Teach us to discern between what is truly good and what is merely almost good. Grant us sharp spiritual sight to discern all things through Your Spirit. Give us wisdom to recognize deception, courage to reject what is contrary to Your ways, and hearts that love what You love and hate what You hate.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 
Amen.
