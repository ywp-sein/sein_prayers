# 64 Peace to You

*26.08.2026*

"You love righteousness and hate wickedness; Therefore God, Your God, has anointed You With the oil of gladness more than Your companions." 
Psalms 45:7

This is the 65th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Please help us to stand firm when people question our beliefs and faith in You. At that moment, please help us to keep our focus and eyes on You, and not to listen to lies or be distracted by our enemies. Because Your Truth prevails over all things, and Your holy fire consumes all evil, we do not have to tremble.

Please help the people in this city who turn to You to have a heart that loves Your righteousness much more and hates wickedness much more.

Lord, please continue to teach us how to love You more with all our hearts, strength, minds, and souls. Forge us to be good witnesses and show this city and the world that there is a mighty God among us. Grant us that victorious joy and overwhelming  peace when we look at Your cross.

May Your kingdom come.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 
Amen.
