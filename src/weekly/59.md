# 59 Look to the Lamb

*15.07.2026*

I rejoiced, and I still rejoice, that for the very first time last weekend, united sounds of worship filled my home. We all came from different nations, and I was deeply moved as we sang "Look to the Lamb" together. 

This is the 59th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

I thank You for the glimpse of heaven I have seen in my home, where people from different nations gathered together to worship You.

May more households become houses of prayer and temples of worship for You, the one true God, throughout this city.

Please bring a breakthrough to this city.<br>
Make our hearts clean, <br>
so that we may sincerely sing from the depths of our hearts:

"Worthy is the Lamb who was slain<br>
To receive power and riches and wisdom,<br>
And strength and honor and glory and blessing!

Blessing and honor and glory and power<br>
Be to You who sits on the throne,<br>
And to the Lamb, forever and ever!"

Help us to always keep our eyes on You.
And please, please fill this city with the hope of Your return.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, <br>
Amen.
