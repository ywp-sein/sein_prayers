# 68 Make the Godless Godly Again 

*16.09.2026*

I am really grateful for a very fruitful conversation and prayer time today. Besides encountering a church I had never noticed before, I also got to know that the most godless place in the world is actually near Berlin.

This is the 68th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

I thank You today for the fruitful conversations and the connections You have brought into our lives. Please continue to guide us and give us instructions so that we may know how to take the next step.

I thank You for the people in Berlin who are pursuing You. I also thank you for bringing Your children together to pray for Your kingdom and to bring Your love to society.

Lord, I know that what I am about to pray for may seem impossible in human terms, especially when Berlin is still filled with all kinds of ungodly things. Yet, I still want to pray that one day, You would use Berlin to reach out to its neighbor, one of the most godless places in the world.

I ask You to prepare Berlin for this calling. Please bring hope and Your love to this society, so that people there may truly see that You are among us. May You stir within them a curiosity and a longing for the Love we have come to know.

Lord, give us the persistent heart to pray without ceasing, to pray in Your Spirit, and to pray with hope. Help us to pray for the renewal of our own hearts, because that is the key to bringing changes to our surroundings.

Help us to pray for our neighbors and our surroundings. Give us compassion and the courage to be bold, so that, whether in season or out of season, we may pray and preach the gospel and make Your name known to those around us.

Lord, I ask You for a miracle: please make the most godless place on earth godly again.

In Your holy name I pray, 

Amen.
