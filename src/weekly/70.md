# 70 Worship and Praise

A surprising encounter at church. The fellowship time after the service led to a great reminder: “We can do nothing on our own. Jesus relied on the Father and did only what Father wanted. The Holy Spirit speaks only what He hears. Why shouldn’t we do the same?”

Thank you for sharing.

This is the 70th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Teach us how to praise You. Teach us how to prepare our hearts to worship You. Help us not to worship and praise You in our own strength, but with the simplicity of being Your children, knowing the truth that we belong to You. Help us simply come to You and praise You.

Lord, I specifically ask that You teach us not only to worship You with our voices, but also to worship You with our lives. Teach us to present ourselves as living sacrifices and to allow You to renew our minds daily.

Father, please gather us together and unite us in worshiping You. Teach us the power of worshiping You and the power of exalting Your name above every other name.

Lord, teach us about Your holiness. What does it mean? What does Your holiness have to do with our lives? How can we build a closer relationship with You?

Holy Spirit, help us to pray day and night, and let the incense of our prayers arise before Your throne. Guide us in the way of righteousness, for You are the Way.

Father, I also ask You to bless all those who are spreading the gospel throughout the city and who are willing to worship You publicly. I ask You to reward them by revealing more of Yourself to them, because You Yourself are our great reward.
Please be with them, and let Your presence fill their worship. Bless those who sincerely desire to make disciples and spread the gospel throughout the city. Strengthen them, encourage them, and draw them closer to You as they serve You.

Father, bless them, and let Your presence go with them wherever they go. May everything they do bring glory to Your name.

In Your holy name I pray, 

Amen.
