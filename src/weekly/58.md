# 58 Arise Souls

*08.07.2026*

"My God is reconciled;<br>
His pardoning voice I hear;<br>
He owns me for His child;<br>
I can no longer fear.<br>
With confidence I now draw nigh,<br>
And, 'Father, Abba, Father,' cry."<br>
----Hymn by Charles Wesley

This is the 58th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Arise the soul,<br>
Arise the cast-down soul.

Deep calls unto deep at the noise of Your waterfalls;<br>
All Your waves and billows have gone over.

Yet, <br>
You are reconciled.<br>
May Your pardoning voice be heard.<br>
Own the soul as Your child.<br>
Let there be no longer fear,<br>
But confidence to draw nigh,<br>
To draw near with confidence,<br>
And cry, "Father, Abba, Father."

Command Your steadfast love by day; <br>
Be with us in Your songs by night.

Help the soul to hope in God, <br>
For there shall yet be praise to You, <br>
Our salvation and our God.

As the deer pants for streams of water, <br>
may our souls pant for You.

Arise our souls.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, <br>
Amen.
