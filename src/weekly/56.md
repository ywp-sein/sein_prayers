# 56 Breath and Water

*24.06.2026*

Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.
-- John 3:5 

This is the 56th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

If the coming heat is Your response to the proud,<br>
then please pour down even more of Your refreshing living water<br>
upon the humble. 

Please clothe Your children with steadfast love,<br>
strengthen them with patient endurance,<br>
and teach them to number their days as they wait<br>
for the precious fruit of Your season. 

And Lord, I ask for Your mercy,

May Your breath blow upon hearts, <br>
bringing both conviction and comfort.<br>
Lead the proud to repentance;<br>
guide the humble to refreshment. 

May Your Word be written upon hearts,<br>
and may those who receive Your breath<br>
love You more tomorrow than they do today,<br>
day by day.

In Your holy name I pray and receive, 
Amen.
