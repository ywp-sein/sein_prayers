# 64 Awaken Conscience

*19.08.2026*

“If you love someone who is ruining his or her life because of faulty thinking, and you don't do anything about it because you are afraid of what others might think, it would seem that rather than being loving, you are in fact being heartless.” 
― William Wilberforce

"If anyone, then, knows the good they ought to do and doesn’t do it, it is sin for them."
― James 4:17 

This is the 64th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Have mercy on Your children. Restore our image of justice. Lord, sadly, there are people out there who have a distorted image of You and Your justice. And Lord, please help them to understand that justice is also love, because You care about what is broken. You are also willing to restore and even redeem.

So Lord, I really ask You to restore this missing piece in many people's lives, so that they can see You clearly. At the same time, help them to see themselves clearly, so that they can truly turn away from sin, because You hate sin. Sin is what separates us from Your presence.

Please continue to show us grace and help us to reflect on our own problems, surrender everything to You, and reflect more deeply on how we can help others. Awaken our consciences and arise within our souls. Help us not to live for ourselves, but to love You, to fear You, to be afraid of Your absence, and to have hearts that desire to pursue Your presence.

And please keep us in Your love and in Your presence. Please restore this missing piece in this city, in this society, and in our surroundings. But humble us and renew our minds first. Help us to make surrender the first thing we do. Help us not to lack zeal, but keep us fervently serving You, 

Lord, show us Your love and justice in a magnificent and glorious way. 

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 
Amen.
