# 66 A Great, United, and Fierce Fire

*02.09.2026*

"Now announce to the army, 'Anyone who trembles with fear may turn back and leave Mount Gilead.' "
Judges 7:3a

This is the 66th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Have mercy on our shallow faith. I am tired of asking You to ignite fires in people again and again. Millions of small fires can be extinguished by a strong wind, but a great fire only grows stronger when the winds come. Lord, that is what I want. I don't want millions of small fires. I want a fire that continues to burn—one united fire that grows stronger and bigger, a great fire that cannot easily be extinguished, and a fierce fire that eventually spreads throughout the city.

Lord, if a person needs to be ignited again and again, perhaps that person has not yet become truly burnable. So, please prune us and prepare us. Separate the wet wood from the dry. Burn away everything in us that we refuse to surrender to You. Call Your people out and bring them out of Egypt. Give them the wisdom to manifest Your victory in Babylon and the discernment to recognize that there is another Person with us in the fire—and that Person is You.

Lord, help us. Remove from the pulpits of this city those who are not faithfully preaching Your Word. Remove them, and gather Your sheep again through true shepherds—shepherds who will faithfully lead, nourish, and care for Your people.

Lord, I ask You to provide these shepherds with everything they need: the heart to care, the strength to persevere, the wisdom to lead, and the provision to faithfully serve those who are lost and unsaved in this city. Let their lives burn with a genuine and enduring fire for You, and through them, let that fire spread throughout this city.

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, I pray and receive, 
Amen.
