# 71 The Lord Is My Judge

*07.10.2026*

Last weekend, I visited a friend's place twice.

On my first visit, I was given a cup for my drink. What caught my attention was that the cup had "Bundesministerium für Justiz" written on it. That evening, I felt led to walk home. On my way home, I noticed the German Constitution hanging on a wall. It was then that I realized that the place I was passing by was actually the "Bundesministerium für Justiz."

On my second visit, while having coffee and cake with some German neighbors of my friends, I was given the same cup again.

This is the 71th weekly prayer:

Dear Heavenly Father,

Thank You for seeing the poor, the injustice, and the weak in this city. I know You are a just and loving God, and for Your name's sake, You won't be silent about any of them. And please help Your followers in this city to talk of Your righteous help all day long. Help us to give thanks to You that the poor can say they are rich in You, and the weak can say they are strong in You.

Please give us the heart of Elijah to keep praying and the heart of the widow who kept asking the unjust judge, so that we may keep asking for the things You want us to pray for.

Lord, I have asked for Your justice, and I still want to ask. Just as You turned over the tables in the temple thousands of years ago, do the same today. Please remove all the profane offerings to You in this city, for You are the same God yesterday, today, and forever, and You alone are holy.

Thank You for prompting us to pray for impossible things by humbling us to know that without abiding in You, we cannot do anything good, and without Your power, all efforts are in vain. Only You can do impossible things.

I ask for Your justice in all the unjust things in this city.

In Your mighty name I pray, 

Amen.
