# 75 Tear Down the Walls

*04.11.2026*

Last Sunday at church, I talked to a lady who witnessed the Berlin Wall and experienced having relatives on the other side of the wall. Weeks ago, I also met people whose church had been separated because of the wall, and people couldn't go to church because of it.

This is the 75th weekly prayer.

Dear Heavenly Father,

I pray that You tear down the walls that cause division and separation. Like what You did to the walls of Jericho, tear them down. And help us to believe that You have delivered them into our hands.

No matter what they are, any walls that hinder people from being close to You and united in this city, tear them all down.

Whoever tries to rebuild these walls, please let them face the consequences, as those who tried to rebuild Jericho did. But have mercy on them, and use it to turn them to You.

Whoever tries to hide unclean things under their tents, please let them face the consequences, as those who tried to hide these unclean things and brought curses upon their people did. But have mercy on them, and use it to turn them to You.

Lord, please tear these walls down and even smash them into dust with Your mighty hands. Turn over the tables that don't honor You, as You did in the temple thousands of years ago.

Let the strong wind of the Holy Spirit blow away dust and ashes, and breath new lives into this city.

Father, please exalt Your name above all things in this city.

There should be only one God in Berlin, and it is You.

In the name of the Father, of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,
Amen.
